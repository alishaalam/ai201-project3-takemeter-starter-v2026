wants_plan: The poster describes their own goal or problem and asks for steps, tactics or ideas to act on, such as how to get customers, how to start something, or how to handle a situation.
wants_answer: The poster asks for one specific item or fact, such as a recommended tool, vendor, supplier or professional, or a definite legal, tax or compliance answer.
wants_verdict: The poster puts a specific idea, option or decision on the table and asks whether it is good, right or worth doing.
wants_peer_input: The poster asks the community to share experiences, practices or opinions, or whether others are in the same situation, with no method, item or verdict they need to act on.
